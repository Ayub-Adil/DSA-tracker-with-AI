Rules for this site

AI warning: the AI can be wrong. Always check hints, pseudocode and quiz answers yourself (for example on LeetCode) before you trust them.

AI usage limits

  • You must be signed in to use the AI.
  • Each person gets 15 AI requests per day. Hint, Review my note, Quiz, Quiz me and Study plan all count toward the same 15.
  • The limit resets at midnight UTC (5:30 AM India time).
  • The AI is a free service. It can be slow, busy, or stop working without notice.
  • Please don't spam the buttons. Accounts that abuse the AI may be limited or removed.

What the AI can see

  • Hint and Quiz send the question name. Quiz me sends the names of some questions you solved. Review my note sends your note. Study plan sends your topic progress.
  • Don't write personal or private information in your notes.
  • The AI provider processes this under its own terms, and a free plan may allow it to use requests to improve its service.

Your account and data

  • When you sign in, your name, email, solved questions, solve times, stars and notes are saved online in your account so they sync between devices.
  • The site owner can view members' progress (name, email, solved count and topics).
  • Verify your email when you register, and don't share your password.
  • "Continue without account" saves only in this browser. Clearing browser data erases it, and the AI is not available.
  • Use Backup / Restore to keep your own copy.

About the questions

  • Easy, Medium and Hard tags were assigned from memory and can be wrong. Check the label on LeetCode.
  • Some LeetCode problems need LeetCode Premium. "Practice" links open a web search.
  • This is a personal practice tool. It is not connected to LeetCode, NeetCode or Striver (takeUforward).
  • Try each problem yourself first. Use hints to get unstuck, not to copy.
